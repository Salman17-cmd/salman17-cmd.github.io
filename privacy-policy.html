Empire Avenue — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 03 September 2026

What is kept on your device

Your progress — coins, gems, unlocked boards, dice and tokens, and your chest progress — is saved on your phone. It is not sent anywhere unless you sign in.

What is sent to our server

Only if you choose to sign in. We then send an account identifier that your device generated at random, a secret used to prove the account is yours, and the display name you chose. We do not read any hardware identifier from your phone.

While you play an online match, the server holds what the match needs — the seat you are in, your name, and the state of the game — so that the match survives a disconnection or a server restart.

Facebook sign-in

If you sign in with Facebook, Facebook gives us a login token, which our server uses only to confirm the sign-in, and your Facebook user id, which is used only to show your profile picture. We do not post anything, and we do not read your friends, posts or contacts.

Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded adverts: you may watch one to earn a temporary bonus. Nothing in the game requires watching an advert, and no advert is shown unless you ask for one.

The adverts are served by Google AdMob. To serve them, Google and its advertising partners may use your device's advertising identifier, your approximate location as derived from your IP address, and information about your device. What they may use, and whether adverts are personalised, depends on the choice you make in the consent form the game shows you. You can change that choice at any time from the game's privacy option. Google's own explanation of how it uses this data is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

When you finish watching a rewarded advert, Google tells our server which account earned the reward, so that the bonus can be added. Nothing else about the advert reaches us.

What we do not collect

No precise location, no contacts, no photos, no microphone or camera access. There is nothing in the game that is bought with real money.

Deleting your data

Signing out removes the account from your device. To have the account deleted from our server, write to sadqq.salman@gmail.com and we will remove it.

Children

This game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
